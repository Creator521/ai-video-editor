# Lemo-Opuscar film direction

Use the Lemo-Opuscar library as a style and directing reference for this illustrated documentary project. The current app builds scenes from narration and stills, then adds camera movement and mixes audio; adapt a named visual style to those capabilities instead of assuming the library's renderer is installed.

## Workflow

1. Ask for or infer topic, target duration, language, intended audience, and visual style. Offer 2–3 styles when none is specified.
2. Write a treatment before producing scenes: narrative arc, chapter rhythm, visual motifs, sound plan, and a small set of key shots. Keep historical claims tied to research.
3. Turn the treatment into scene-level narration and image prompts. Keep composition, palette, lighting, period detail, and camera movement coherent across scenes.
4. Preview representative scenes and review historical details, legibility, pacing, audio levels, and visual consistency before final export.
5. Deliver the rendered MP4 and the script, scene assets, and attribution/research records used to make it.

## Reference library

The upstream skill's full workflow depends on the Lemo-Opuscar library, its guides, style prompts, and rendering tools. If that library is available locally, read its `AGENTS.md`, `DIRECTOR.md`, `TECHNIQUE.md`, and the chosen `STYLE.md` and follow those instructions. Do not claim the library or its render dependencies are installed just because this guide exists.
